Stabilize

Launching products is tough as a lot of unknowns can surface and a lot of unwanted things happen. Every product needs that nurturing touch as it takes its first few steps.

  • Funnel Optimizer

    Fix the metrics across the usage funnel

    The first few signs that something is not working comes from looking at the key metrics across the product funnel. We look at the funnel metrics of the user journey in focus and investigate with real users to identify opportunities for improvement. We apply ergonomics and psychology to bring experience improvements at the relevant points across the product funnel to achieve a significantly optimized & stable funnel.

  • Growth Pod

    Get dedicated UX bandwidth to grow

    As the product stabilizes, its key to think of growth. Getting that extra bandwidth to build out those features that would help grow the products helps. Get our UX team to help out and place your product on the growth trajectory.

  • Experience Mentorship

    Guidance by an industry seasoned UX expert

    An outside experienced perspective that brings in a blend of product & design understanding can drive internal design & product teams to solve tough problems and push boundaries of innovation. We provide our experienced executive staff to guide your internal teams on areas ranging from product strategy, research processes, innovation, design-build, and metric-led design.

Launching products is precarious

Never has it been tougher to make products succeed. Complacency can make products crash and die really fast. Companies need to solve metric drops fast, make alterations to value propositions, and build in features that will create more engagement. We grow our own techniques and practices that are specially focused towards helping products to succeed.
