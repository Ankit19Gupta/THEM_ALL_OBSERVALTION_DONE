IT'S DIFFERENT but IT'S SAME! Google's M3 Expressive vs iOS 26 Liquid Glass.

  • UX
  • Business centric design
  • Jul 21, 2025
  • 5 Min read
Share this article

We’re officially in a new era of design — one that feels more human than ever. Not just functional. Not just aesthetic. But expressive, emotional, and alive.

With Google’s Material Design 3: Expressive and Apple’s Liquid Glass in iOS 26, both ecosystems are embracing a core truth: Interfaces should feel — not just function.

What’s similar?

Both systems reflect a shift toward:

  • Emotionally-driven design

  • Meaningful depth, motion, and translucency

  • Personalization and adaptability

  • Creating experiences that connect, not just guide

This marks a significant moment where design is not just about structures anymore but also about the experience.

What’s different?

Material Design 3: Expressive brings bold customization, dynamic theming, and shapes that adapt to user identity. It’s about designing for you.

iOS Liquid Glass, on the other hand, creates a sense of immersive elegance with soft transparency, light play, and organic motion. It responds to you.

Different philosophies. Different visual languages. But the same intent to make digital interfaces feel human.

PS: While I truly appreciate Apple’s intent with Liquid Glass, I personally foresee some usability and accessibility issues, the constant translucency, lighting shifts, and motion look elegant, but might become a distraction or even a barrier for many users, let's hope Apple has tested all this well!

Also, let’s be honest it feels a bit like a very polished reboot of Windows Vista Aero Glass. Aesthetically familiar, functionally… questionable.

As designers, this is a moment to rethink how we create products that don’t just look good — but feel right, for everyone.

We’re not just building screens. We’re designing emotion, personality, presence and for business to grow.
